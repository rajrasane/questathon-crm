# Three-minute walkthrough

Record the final hosted URL in a private browser window after deployment acceptance. Keep credentials, environment files, terminals and unrelated tabs out of frame. The captioned local screencast is a review draft until that acceptance is complete.

| Time | Show | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Overview and account selector | “Account Brief helps an account owner understand the next decision. It fetches source records through MCP, stores them, and turns them into a sourced commercial position, people map and three proposed actions.” |
| 0:20–0:40 | Three commercial values | “Existing annual contract value is separate from opportunity value and new booked value. Missing values remain unknown. An open offer or discovered opportunity is not presented as a newly won deal.” |
| 0:40–1:05 | Expand first action, then latest signal | “Each priority includes a proposed owner and date, its reason, and the intended outcome. Incoming signals can change the ranking. Recommendations remain proposals rather than customer commitments.” |
| 1:05–1:25 | Open action evidence and record details | “Supporting records are one click away. Claims, reported statements, verified evidence and interpretations remain distinguishable. Here is the underlying source and its provenance.” |
| 1:25–1:45 | People view; expand assessment | “Decision, budget, operations and dependencies are distinct roles. A job title or previous signature does not prove current authority. The assessment tells us what still needs confirmation.” |
| 1:45–2:05 | Deals view; expand opportunity | “Every opportunity has its own stage, value, blockers, deadline and evidence. Unlinked account offers remain separate; no currency is invented.” |
| 2:05–2:30 | Switch account; open conflicting evidence | “Both accounts use the same rules. Conflicting statements remain side by side. A projection or savings claim needs reconciliation with the underlying model and finance evidence.” |
| 2:30–2:50 | Open questions, risk or usage analysis | “Unknowns have a resolution path. Operational patterns identify what to investigate without guessing the cause. The interface reveals detail when needed rather than displaying every document at once.” |
| 2:50–3:00 | Data status and overview | “The token stays on the server. One coordinated caller respects the request limit, caches the baseline, and reanalyzes new signals. The result is a traceable next action, with honest uncertainty.” |

If hosting is not verified at recording time, say “local preview” explicitly. Do not claim a newly arriving live signal was observed unless it actually occurred; mocked changed-signal tests establish tested behavior only.
